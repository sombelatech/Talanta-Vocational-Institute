Our History & Establishment

Talanta Vocational Institute is a private vocational training institute established on 23rd April 2026, registered and operating under NACTVET/TVET. We are dedicated to bridging the local skills gap by providing high-quality, industry-relevant practical education.

We are located in Mbagala Town, Mbagala Zakhem, Kurasini Mji Mpya, Temeke District, Dar es Salaam — a strategic commercial area with a large population, high youth unemployment, and strong demand for specialized skills.

Departmental Structure

Talanta Vocational Institute is organized into seven core operational departments, each responsible for a specific area of institutional performance, quality, and service delivery.

  1. Office of the Principal – Strategy, Planning and Projects Department
  2. Academic, Training, Research and Innovation Department
  3. Quality Assurance, Regulatory Compliance and Institutional Effectiveness Department
  4. Student Affairs, Admissions, Registration and Student Support Department
  5. Corporate Services Department – Finance, HR, Administration and Procurement
  6. Infrastructure, Workshops, ICT, Estates, Health, Safety and Environment Department
  7. Business Development, Marketing, Industry Linkages, Employment, Entrepreneurship and International Relations Department

Key Management Committees

Talanta's governance system is supported by ten permanent management committees, each with clear membership, authority, work plans, minutes, action registers, KPIs, risk responsibilities and reporting obligations.

  1. Institutional Governing / Management Committee
  2. Academic, Curriculum and Training Committee
  3. Quality Assurance and Quality Control Committee
  4. Examinations, Assessment and Certification Committee
  5. Admissions, Registration and Student Records Committee
  6. Finance, Audit, Procurement and Risk Management Committee
  7. Human Resources, Staff Development and Performance Committee
  8. Infrastructure, Workshop Equipment, ICT, Health, Safety and Environment Committee
  9. Student Welfare, Discipline, Guidance and Inclusiveness Committee
  10. Industry Linkages, Employment, Entrepreneurship, Innovation and Community Engagement Committee

Committee Purpose

The committees provide institutional oversight across teaching, quality, safety, compliance, student support, finance, procurement, human resources, industry engagement, and continuous improvement. In line with strong TVET governance practice, each committee must have defined terms of reference, leadership, meeting schedules, minutes, action logs, KPI tracking, risk responsibilities and reporting obligations.

Management Meeting Cycle

  • Daily: operational coordination in key areas such as admissions, academics, workshops, finance, student affairs and safety.
  • Weekly: Senior Management Meeting chaired by the Principal to review student numbers, teaching, attendance, finance, staff, workshops, marketing, admissions, complaints and urgent risks.
  • Monthly: each department and committee reports on performance, progress and corrective actions.
  • Quarterly: institutional performance review covering student recruitment, retention, academic results, staff, revenue, expenditure, equipment, quality, compliance and industry linkages.
  • Annually: strategic review and annual operational planning against the institutional strategic plan.
