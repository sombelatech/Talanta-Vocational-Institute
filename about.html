Our History & Establishment

Talanta Vocational Institute is a private vocational training institute established on 23rd April 2026, registered and operating under NACTVET/TVET. We are dedicated to bridging the local skills gap by providing high-quality, industry-relevant practical education.

We are located in Mbagala Town, Mbagala Zakhem, Kurasini Mji Mpya, Temeke District, Dar es Salaam — a strategic commercial area with a large population, high youth unemployment, and strong demand for specialized skills.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

VISION

"To be the leading gateway for bridging the skills gap for a brighter tomorrow in Tanzania."

MISSION

"To build a brighter tomorrow for individuals through hands-on skills training and career pathways."

MOTTO

"To transform everybody to master the production hands-on skills in order to command the future."

Business Goals:

  • To offer the best training services to the community
  • To offer mentorship services to the community
  • To offer advisory services to the community
  • To offer support services to the community

Core Values

Community Welfare · Quality Services · Knowledge and Skills · Expertise · Commitment · Reliability · Trust · Transparency · Honesty · Accountability · Community Satisfaction
