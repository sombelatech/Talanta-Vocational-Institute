Business & Entrepreneurship

Basic Entrepreneurship

Basic Requirements: Basic reading, writing, arithmetic, communication, creativity and willingness to start a business.

Entry Qualification: Generally primary/secondary education or equivalent practical experience.

Usefulness: Helps young people, graduates and informal-sector workers identify business opportunities, prepare business plans, price products and manage customers.

Employment Opportunities: Entrepreneurship training centres, NGOs, SME-support programmes, business development organizations.

Self-Employment Potential: Very high — retail, service, consultancy, agency, online business, or combined with a technical skill.

Why It Matters Today: Converts vocational skills into income-generating enterprises and supports employment creation.

What You'll Learn: Business planning, costing, pricing, marketing, bookkeeping, customer care, taxation basics, digital marketing, business ethics.
