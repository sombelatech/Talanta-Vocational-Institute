Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to apply?
No — our programmes are designed for beginners as well as those looking to formalize existing skills.
How long are the training programmes?
Programme duration varies by trade. Contact admissions for the specific schedule of your chosen programme.
Is there financial aid or a payment plan?
Payment plan options are being finalized. Reach out to our admissions team directly to discuss what's available.
Do you help with job placement after graduation?
Yes — we facilitate internships and connect graduates with employer partners as part of our mission.
Can I apply if I'm not from Dar es Salaam?
Yes, Talanta welcomes students from across Tanzania.
